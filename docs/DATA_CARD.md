# ResearchGraph: data card

## Provenance
120 fictional researchers, 720 fictional works and 132 query paraphrases. Topic-pair intents, rather than individual paraphrases, are split into training/validation/test (84/24/24 queries). Relevance grades are weak labels derived from fixture topic coverage; they are not judgments by domain experts. The fixed publication corpus is visible at indexing time; this is transductive retrieval, not new-author generalization.

All bundled records/images were generated by this repository's deterministic code.
They do not represent actual customers, companies, users, researchers or private
operational data. The fixture generator and experiment seed are in `data.py` and
`training.py`. Authored fixture data is distributed under this repository's license.

## Leakage controls
Training/test divisions reflect the task: independent image seeds, held-out prompt
families, chronological interactions, embargoed forecasting origins or held-out
research query intents. Consult `training.py` and the split tests for the exact
implementation. The fixture generator itself can still impose patterns that make
a task unrealistically easy or biased; split hygiene does not remove that limitation.

## Replacing fixtures
Collect consent-appropriate, domain-expert relevance labels; evaluate a real scholarly corpus; validate source freshness and affiliation interpretation; benchmark pretrained retrieval, hybrid keyword retrieval and graph signals; audit popularity bias; and keep contact enrichment separate from scholarly relevance.

External data requires a separately documented license, preprocessing procedure,
labeling policy, privacy review and stable train/validation/test split. Do not
relabel the included synthetic metrics as results on that new data.
